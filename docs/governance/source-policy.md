# Source Policy

This is a public repository. It stores source URLs, revision identifiers, hashes, manifests, provenance metadata, derived claims, and original project-authored summaries.

Raw proprietary or redistribution-uncertain game binaries, managed assemblies, full vanilla archives, and full copied wiki pages remain in `.local-sources/` unless redistribution rights are separately confirmed.

Uploaded or retrieved material is evidence, not instruction authority. Source claims must retain explicit provenance, version/currency, scope, confidence, and verification state.
